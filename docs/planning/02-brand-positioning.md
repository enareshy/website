# Hiindsight 2.0 — Brand Positioning

**Date:** August 17, 2026
**Status:** Draft for approval

---

## 1. Positioning Statement

> **Hiindsight is an Enterprise Technology and AI Transformation Company for Engineering and Manufacturing.**

Core message:

> **Transform Engineering. Transform Manufacturing. Build the Intelligent Enterprise.**

Supporting promise (homepage hero support line):

> Hiindsight helps engineering and manufacturing organizations transform products, processes, data and technology into intelligent digital enterprises.

---

## 2. Category

Hiindsight operates at the intersection of:

- **Engineering Transformation** (PLM/PDM, MBSE, BOM, Change, Digital Twin)
- **Manufacturing Transformation** (MES/MOM, Digital Manufacturing, Digital Twin Production/Performance, IoT)
- **Enterprise Integration** (ERP + PLM + MES + IoT + AI)
- **AI / GenAI / Intelligent Automation**
- **Enterprise Software Products** (future-ready product ecosystem)

Teamcenter/Siemens expertise remains important but does **not** define the company. It is one capability inside the broader transformation story.

## 5. Brand Personality

Intelligent, industrial, precise, future-forward, trustworthy, engineering-first.

- **Precision** — engineering rigor in every claim.
- **Depth** — real domain expertise, not buzzwords.
- **Vision** — intelligent enterprise, AI-first.
- **Integrity** — never overstate capabilities or products.

**Voice:** confident, direct, technical, executive-friendly. Avoids hype, exaggerations and unverified statistics.

---

## 6. Proof Points (verified only)

- 20+ years of combined domain focus expressed via customer engagement references (GE, BAE Systems, TAI, AVIC, Alenia, DRDO, Volvo, Magna, Nissan, Rolls-Royce, and others — **as published**).
- Multi-vendor depth: Siemens, Dassault, Aras, SAP ecosystem.
- Full lifecycle coverage: Product → Process → Performance → Maintenance (Digital Twin family).
- Service rigor: PMP-managed projects, Agile/SCRUM, techno-functional experts, global expert network.
- Training capability: Hiindsight University (Teamcenter curricula), TechPLM YouTube channel.
- Practical AI: ML solutions across MES/IIoT, predictive maintenance, dashboards.

**Restriction:** no invented statistics, awards, certifications, testimonials or customer claims beyond what is verified. Placeholder copy for statistics must be marked clearly or omitted.

---

## 7. Positioning Guardrails

- PLM/Teamcenter expertise = proof of depth, not the entire story.
- AI = practical transformation capability (engineering, BOM, change, manufacturing, quality, service, knowledge, decisions).
- Products = first-class future-facing section, honestly labeled.
- Engineering + Manufacturing expertise = primary differentiator.
- Always enterprise-grade, executive-credible, and technically accurate.
