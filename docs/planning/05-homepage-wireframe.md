# Hiindsight 2.0 — Homepage Wireframe

**Date:** August 17, 2026
**Status:** Draft for approval

---

## Page Purpose

Communicate Hiindsight's positioning within **5 seconds**, tell the transformation story, and drive conversion via **Start Your Transformation** / **Explore Our Capabilities**.

---

## Section-by-Section Wireframe

### 1. Top Utility Bar (non-scrolling or slim scrollaway)
- About · Careers · Contact (text links) | Language/region placeholder (future)

### 2. Header (sticky)
- Logo (light/dark variant by scroll) · Primary Nav · **Start Your Transformation** CTA · Mobile hamburger

### 3. HERO
- **H1:** BUILD THE INTELLIGENT ENTERPRISE
- **Support:** Hiindsight helps engineering and manufacturing organizations transform products, processes, data and technology into intelligent digital enterprises.
- **CTAs:** [Start Your Transformation] [Explore Our Capabilities]
- **Visual:** Sophisticated technical visualization — connected system grid / data-flow / digital thread animation (reduced-motion aware). No stock photos.
- **Trust strip (optional, verified):** engagement references as subtle logo/text marks (client-approved subset).

### 4. THE TRANSFORMATION GAP
- **Headline:** The Transformation Gap
- **Pain grid (6 cards):** Legacy systems · Data silos · Disconnected processes · Complex BOMs · Manual work & poor integration · Technical debt / slow engineering-manufacturing cycles
- **Close line:** "Most enterprises don't need more tools. They need a connected way to work."

### 5. THE DIGITAL THREAD (animated visualization)
- Horizontal journey: **Concept → Design → Engineering → BOM → Change → Manufacturing → Service**
- Support band: **PLM + ERP + MES + IoT + AI + Digital Twin**
- Value band: **Data → Context → Intelligence → Automation → Decisions**
- Animation: node-to-node data flow, subtle, respects prefers-reduced-motion.

### 6. CAPABILITIES
- 8 cards (from /transform + /technology):
  PLM Transformation · Digital Manufacturing · Digital Thread · AI & GenAI · Digital Twin · Enterprise Integration · Engineering Automation · Product Development
- Each links to relevant hub page.

### 7. AI-FIRST FUTURE
- **Headline:** The AI-First Enterprise
- **AI application grid (verified scope):** AI in Engineering · BOM · Design · Engineering Changes · Manufacturing · Quality · Service · Knowledge · Decision-making
- Tie each AI use case to a real Hiindsight capability (ML for MES/IIoT, predictive maintenance, etc.). No invented claims.

### 8. PRODUCTS (future-ready ecosystem)
- Intro: "Products on a journey from services to platforms."
- Status badges: **Available / In Development / Coming Soon**
- Only confirmed products marked Available (see Decisions Required).
- CTA: **Explore Products**

### 9. WHY HIINSIGHT
- 4–5 proof blocks: Verified experience · Engineering expertise · Enterprise transformation · Technology capability · AI & product thinking
- Optionally a stat row — **only verified numbers or client-approved placeholders**.

### 10. CASE STUDIES (featured)
- 2–3 featured case studies using the reusable structure:
  Challenge → Transformation → Solution → Technology → Implementation → Business Impact
- Links to /insights/case-studies.

### 11. INDUSTRIES (optional strip)
- Automotive · Aerospace & Defence · Energy · Heavy Machinery · Industrial Manufacturing (card links)

### 12. CONTACT / CONVERSION BAND
- **Talk to an Expert** / **Discuss Your Challenge**
- Inline link to /contact with the simple form (Name, Company, Business Email, Role, Industry, Challenge, optional Phone).

### 13. FOOTER
- Full footer nav (see Navigation doc) + legal + contact details.

---

## Conversion Paths

| Visitor | Path |
|---|---|
| Executive | Hero → Why Hiindsight → Case Studies → Contact |
| Architect/Engineer | Hero → Digital Thread → Capabilities → Technology/Solutions |
| Industry-focused | Industries → Solution → Case Study → Contact |
| Product-curious | Products → status view → Contact for trial/access |
