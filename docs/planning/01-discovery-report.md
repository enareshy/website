## 3. Opportunity Gap

The current site positions Hiindsight as a **PLM services firm**. The brief requires positioning Hiindsight as an **Enterprise Technology & AI Transformation company for Engineering and Manufacturing** — spanning consulting, transformation, solutions, products and AI.

**Brand evolution to communicate:**

Consulting → Transformation → Solutions → Products → AI-powered Enterprise Technology

**New site objectives:**

1. Establish engineering + manufacturing + AI authority in 5 seconds.
2. Convert executives (CTO/CIO/CDO/VP) while remaining credible to architects/engineers.
3. Make the product ecosystem a first-class, future-facing section.
4. Build a reusable, headless-ready content model and design system.
